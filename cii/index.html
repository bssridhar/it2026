Points to note

  • Indexed cost = cost × CII of the year of transfer ÷ CII of the year of acquisition (or 2001-02, if later); improvement likewise from the year of improvement (Explanation to s.48 of the 1961 Act; s.72 of the 2025 Act). Improvements before 01-04-2001 are ignored.
  • Transfers on or after 23-07-2024: long-term gains at 12.5% without indexation. For land or building acquired before 23-07-2024 and transferred by a resident individual or HUF, the tax is the lower of 12.5% without indexation and 20% with indexation (proviso to s.112(1)). Transfers before 23-07-2024: 20% with indexation.
  • Long-term: for transfers on or after 23-07-2024, listed securities and units held for more than 12 months, other assets for more than 24 months. Before that date: 12 months for listed securities, 24 months for unlisted shares and immovable property, 36 months for others.
  • Listed equity (s.112A / s.198): 12.5% above Rs. 1.25 lakh from 23-07-2024 (10% above Rs. 1 lakh earlier); short-term (s.111A / s.196) 20% (15% earlier). No indexation.
  • Surcharge on these gains is limited to 15%; health and education cess 4%. For others (firms, companies, non-residents) enter the surcharge rate. Marginal relief is not worked out.
  • Income-tax Act, 2025 applies from 01-04-2026 ("tax year"). Section numbers of the 2025 Act are taken from the published concordance tables – verify them with the Act / CBDT concordance before quoting.
  • The working is based on the figures entered; verify with the Act, the rules and the facts before relying on it.
