Points to note

  • Formula: Indexed cost of acquisition = Cost of acquisition × CII of the year of transfer ÷ CII of the year in which the asset was first held by the seller or 2001-02, whichever is later. Indexed cost of improvement = Cost of improvement × CII of the year of transfer ÷ CII of the year of improvement. (Section 48, Explanation, Income-tax Act, 1961; section 72, Income-tax Act, 2025.)
  • Base year: 2001-02 = 100 from AY 2018-19, when the Finance Act, 2017 moved the base year from 1981-82. For assets acquired before 01-04-2001 the cost may be taken as the fair market value on 01-04-2001 (section 55(2)(b), Income-tax Act, 1961); improvements made before 01-04-2001 are ignored.
  • Transfers on or after 23-07-2024: the Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024 withdrew indexation for long-term capital gains and set the rate at 12.5%. For land or building acquired before 23-07-2024 and transferred by a resident individual or HUF, the tax is the lower of 12.5% without indexation and 20% with indexation (proviso to section 112(1), Income-tax Act, 1961).
  • Long-term: for transfers on or after 23-07-2024, listed securities and units held for more than 12 months, and other assets held for more than 24 months. Before that date: 12 months for listed securities and equity-oriented units, 24 months for unlisted shares and immovable property, 36 months for other assets.
  • No indexation in any case for long-term gains on listed equity shares and equity-oriented units taxed under section 112A (cost may be stepped up to the FMV on 31-01-2018 instead). Units of specified (debt) mutual funds acquired on or after 01-04-2023 are short-term regardless of the period of holding (section 50AA).
  • Income-tax Act, 2025: applies from 01-04-2026 ("tax year" in place of "previous year" and "assessment year"). The CII for 2026-27 (384) was notified under it.
  • The calculator ignores surcharge, health and education cess, exemptions (sections 54, 54EC, 54F, etc.) and the adjustment against the basic exemption limit. Verify with the Act before relying on the result.
