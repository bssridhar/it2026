GSTR-9 / 9C Working – from GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B

Load the GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B PDFs (downloaded from the GST portal) for the year – one month or all twelve at a time. The tool reads them and prepares the GSTR reconciliation working, month-wise data, a GSTR-9 draft and a books-vs-returns summary for GSTR-9C in Excel. The PDFs are read in this browser only.
