3. Founders and trustees

In the order they should appear in the deed. Founders are listed as the parties executing the deed; trustees appear in the First Trustees table. A person can be both.

4. Objects of the trust

The objects of the firm's format are loaded. Untick to leave one out, edit the wording, or add new ones. Order No.: give 1 to the main object, 2 to the next and so on – the deed prints them in that order as a), b), c)… (objects without a number follow in list order). The heading is optional; objects with the same heading are printed under it as (i), (ii). The "not for profit" and "no activities outside India" objects and the "For removal of doubts" declaration are added at the end automatically.
