Open the DSC Register

Opening it now…

The DSC Register has not been connected yet.

  1. Set it up on Google by following dsc-register/SETUP.md (about 15 minutes, once).
  2. Copy the web-app link Google gives at the end.
  3. Open home-config.js in Notepad and paste the link into dscAppUrl: "", between the quotes.

After that, this card on the home page opens the register directly.
