Form 26AS · AIS · TIS Consolidator

Reads Form 26AS, AIS and TIS PDFs and prepares the income consolidation workbook (Excel) for the computation of total income. The PDFs are read in this browser only.

Statements

Passwords: Form 26AS – date of birth as DDMMYYYY; AIS and TIS – PAN in lowercase followed by DDMMYYYY.

The workbook has four sheets: Income Summary, TDS Credit (26AS), Additional Information and 26AS & AIS Check. "Consider for SOI" takes the 26AS figure if the item is only in 26AS, the AIS/TIS figure if it is only in AIS/TIS, the 26AS figure if both agree and the higher figure if they differ. Sections are mapped under the Income-tax Act, 1961; codes under the Income-tax Act, 2025 are flagged for review. The case file and draft hold the figures read from the statements (not the PDFs). The workbook is a working paper – review it against the statements before use.
