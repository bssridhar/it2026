GSTR-2B Analyser – ITC for GSTR-3B

Load the GSTR-2B Excel downloaded from the GST portal. The tool sorts every invoice, note and amendment into eligible ITC, blocked credit, insurance / motor vehicle items, place-of-supply ineligibility, reverse charge and others, lets you re-mark any line with a reason, and gives the GSTR-2B working and the GSTR-3B figures in Excel. The file is read in this browser only.
