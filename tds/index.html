  • The declaration states that the tax on the estimated total income of the year will be nil. Except for a resident individual aged 60 or more, it does not apply where the income of these kinds for the year exceeds the maximum amount not chargeable to tax.
  • The payer delivers the declarations to the prescribed authority by the 7th of the month following the quarter [393(7)].
  • From 01-04-2027 the declaration may also be furnished electronically to a depository for listed units, interest on securities and dividends [393(6)(b), inserted by the Finance Act, 2026].

Other points

  • Time of deduction: at the time of credit to the payee's account or payment, whichever is earlier, unless the Table says otherwise. A credit to a suspense account is treated as a credit to the payee [393(11)].
  • Tax borne by the payer: the income is grossed up so that, after deduction, it equals the net amount payable [393(10)].
  • Paying on the portal: e-File → e-Pay Tax → select "Income Tax Act, 2025" (for tax year 2026-27 onwards) → New Payment → Pay TDS/TCS (challan ITNS 281N). Section codes are shown according to the residential status of the deductee; up to 20 section codes can be paid in one challan (Minor Head 200).
  • Not covered here: TDS on salary (section 392), the higher rate where PAN is not furnished, surcharge and cess on payments to non-residents, lower-deduction certificates, and the "rates in force", which are in the Finance Act.

Source: section 393, Income-tax Act, 2025 as amended by the Finance Act, 2026 (Income Tax Department). Payment steps: ITNS 281N FAQs, incometax.gov.in.
