Partnership Deed

Prepares the partnership deed in the firm's format, an NOC for the place of business, a data sheet and a request list. Partner details can be read from PAN, Aadhaar and bank statement PDFs, or loaded from Excel. Everything runs in this browser and nothing is uploaded.

1. Partners

A partnership needs at least two partners. A firm can have at most 50 partners (Section 464 of the Companies Act, 2013 read with Rule 10 of the Companies (Miscellaneous) Rules, 2014).
Have the details in Excel instead? Useful when the KYC copies are scanned. Download the template, fill it in and upload it.

2. Firm and deed details

Blank = same as the date of deed.
e.g. "for a period of five years from the date of commencement"
0 = no interest.
Used in the clause on raising loans.
Business of the firm (object clause) Describe the business and the tool suggests a draft from standard clause wording; edit it as needed.

3. Partner details

For each partner, upload the PAN, Aadhaar and bank statement PDFs and the fields fill in automatically (shaded fields came from a document). Check every field. The address is taken from the bank statement. Scanned copies can't be read, so key those details in by hand.
