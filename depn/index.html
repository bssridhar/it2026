    Reminders for the audit of PPE

    • Schedule II Note 4 – a significant part with a different useful life is depreciated separately (component accounting).
    • Note 2 – depreciation from the date the asset is put to use (ready for use), not the date of invoice; check the installation / commissioning records for additions near the year-end.
    • Note 6 – extra shift depreciation only for the days actually worked on double / triple shift, and never for assets marked NESD; see the production / shift records.
    • Useful life and residual value reviewed at least at each financial year-end (Ind AS 16, para 51; AS 10 (Revised)); a change is a change in accounting estimate.
    • Part B – a life or residual value notified by a regulatory authority under an Act of Parliament or by the Central Government overrides Part C.
    • CARO 2020, para 3(i): (a) proper records with full particulars, quantitative details and situation of PPE (and of intangible assets); (b) physical verification at reasonable intervals and treatment of material discrepancies; (c) title deeds of immovable property in the name of the company; (d) revaluation – whether by a registered valuer, and the amount if the change is 10% or more of the net carrying value of a class; (e) proceedings under the Benami Transactions (Prohibition) Act, 1988.
    • Profit or loss on sale / discard: compare with the sale invoices, and the GST on the sale of the used asset.

    Part C – useful lives, with the equivalent rates

    From the text of Schedule II to the Companies Act, 2013 (Part C) used for this tool; NESD = no extra shift depreciation. SLM rate = (100% − residual %) ÷ life, on cost. WDV rate = 1 − (residual ÷ cost)1 ÷ life, on the opening written down value. Check the current text of the Schedule for amendments.
