Company Incorporation Documents

For a Private Limited Company or a One Person Company. Generates the NOC for the registered office and a data sheet for SPICe+ (with e-MoA and e-AoA). Details can be read from PAN, Aadhaar and bank statement PDFs, or loaded from Excel. Everything runs in this browser and nothing is uploaded.

1. Type of company and persons

Have the details in Excel instead? Useful when the KYC copies are scanned. Download the template, fill it in and upload it.

2. Company details

Main objects (for e-MoA) Describe the business and the tool suggests a draft from standard clause wording. Edit it as needed; it goes into the data sheet for the e-MoA.

3. Directors, subscribers and nominee

For each person, upload the PAN, Aadhaar and bank statement PDFs and the fields fill in automatically (shaded fields came from a document). Check every field. The address is taken from the bank statement. Scanned copies can't be read, so key those details in by hand.

4. NOC from owner of registered office premises

5. Checklist

Items received from an upload or entered details tick themselves; tick the rest as they come in. Unticked items go into the data sheet and the request list for the client.

Document checks

Upload documents to see the checks here.

Wording of the NOC follows the firm's format. Details read from documents are a convenience, so check each generated document against the original KYC documents and current MCA requirements before it is signed or filed. The Aadhaar number is never read or stored.
