  • Rebate: a resident individual with total income up to Rs. 12,00,000 gets a rebate of the tax, up to Rs. 60,000. Just above Rs. 12,00,000, tax is limited to the income in excess of Rs. 12,00,000 (marginal relief).
  • Surcharge: 10% above Rs. 50 lakh, 15% above Rs. 1 crore, 25% above Rs. 2 crore (the new regime caps it at 25%), with marginal relief at each threshold.
  • Health and education cess: 4% of tax plus surcharge.
  • Standard deduction of Rs. 75,000 for salary and pension income.
  • Total income and tax are rounded to the nearest ten rupees.
  • Not covered: income at special rates (capital gains, lottery, etc.), the old regime, deductions other than the standard deduction and employer's NPS contribution, and relief for arrears. The rebate is not available against tax on income at special rates.
  • The slabs and rebate set by the Finance Act, 2025 continue for tax year 2026-27 under the Income-tax Act, 2025 (the Finance Act, 2026 did not change them). Verify against the Act before relying on the result.
