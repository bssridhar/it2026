# ROC Compliance

Open `index.html` (keep the `shared` folder of the tool kit next to this folder).

First event: **appointment of directors – one to five at a time – in a private limited company** – as an Additional Director by the Board
(s.161(1), regularised at the next general meeting under s.152(2)), or directly at a general meeting (s.152(2)).

1. **Information and documents required** – a list for the client that follows the manner of appointment and whether the
   person has a DIN: copy the text, download a PDF to attach to an e-mail, a Word file, or open it in the e-mail program.
2. **Details** – enter on screen (a tab for each director; add up to five), or download the Excel template, fill it and
   import it: "Details" (company, meetings, signing director), "Directors" (one column per director) and "Other
   interests" (with the director number, for MBP-1 / DIR-8).
3. **Checks** – CIN / DIN / PAN format, notice periods (s.173(3), s.101), DIR-12 due date, AGM due date, limit of
   directorships, etc.
4. **Procedure and timeline** – steps with the provisions and due dates.
5. **Documents** (Word, singly or as a ZIP) – DIR-2, DIR-8, MBP-1, the appointment letter and the DIR-3 data sheet for each
   director; the notices, resolutions (one per director), minutes, DIR-12 data sheet and register entries cover all of them: client checklist, procedure, notice of the Board meeting, certified true copy
   of the Board resolution, draft minutes, DIR-2, DIR-8, MBP-1, letter of appointment, notice of the AGM / EGM with the
   explanatory statement, certified copy of the members' resolution, DIR-12 data sheet (appointment and regularisation),
   register of directors entry, and the DIR-3 data sheet when there is no DIN. Resolutions, notices and letters can be
   printed on the company's letter-head.

All documents are drafts to be checked against the current Act, Rules, Secretarial Standards, the company's Articles and
the e-forms on the MCA portal. The Word template is built by `tools/build_templates.py` and embedded by
`tools/embed_templates.py`; the content is in `lib/compose.js`.
