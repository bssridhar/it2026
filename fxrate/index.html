    Income-tax Rules, 2026 – Rule 206 (rate for conversion of income in foreign currency)

    SBI telegraphic transfer buying rate as on the specified date (Rule 206(1)); "telegraphic transfer buying rate" as defined in Rule 207.

    Sl.Types of incomeSpecified date

    • Rule 206(3): Rule 206(1) does not apply to Sl. 3 income received in, or brought into, India before the specified date.

    Rule 207 (TDS on income payable in foreign currency)

    SBI TT buying rate as on the date on which tax is required to be deducted under Chapter XIX-B; where not published on that date, the last such published rate. Applies to income payable to an assessee outside India, to an IFSC unit, or by an IFSC unit to an assessee in India.

    ICDS VI – Foreign Currency Transactions

    Initial recognition at the rate at the date of the transaction (weekly / monthly average permitted where rates do not fluctuate significantly); monetary items restated at the closing rate at the end of the year; exchange differences recognised as income or expense.

    CGST Rules, rule 34

    (1) Goods: the rate notified by the Board under section 14 of the Customs Act, 1962, for the date of time of supply in terms of section 12. (2) Services: the rate determined as per generally accepted accounting principles for the date of time of supply in terms of section 13.
