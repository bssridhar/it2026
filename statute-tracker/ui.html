New items found

Each item links to the official government website where it was found. Read the notification, circular or order itself (and the Gazette, where applicable) before relying on it. Open the report of the last check.

Statutes and the websites checked

Only websites on the allowed government domains can be added (see Settings).

Allowed government domains

Websites are checked only if their address ends with one of these. Links found on a page that point elsewhere (social media, private websites) are ignored, and a page that redirects elsewhere is not read.
For example gov.in covers incometaxindia.gov.in, cbic-gst.gov.in, esic.gov.in and so on. rbi.org.in is the Reserve Bank of India's own website. Add only official government or regulator domains.

E-mail the daily report (optional)

After each daily check the new items can be e-mailed to you. For Gmail, use an "App password" (Google Account > Security > 2-Step Verification > App passwords), not your normal password. These settings are stored only on this computer, in the data folder.
