LLP Incorporation Documents

Generates the Form 9 consent, consent letter, subscription sheet and NOC for the registered office as Word files. Partner details can be read from PAN, Aadhaar and bank statement PDFs. Everything runs in this browser and nothing is uploaded.

1. Partners in the proposed LLP

An LLP needs at least two partners (Section 6(1)) and at least two designated partners who are individuals, at least one of them resident in India (Section 7(1)), LLP Act, 2008.

2. LLP details

Section 15(1) of the LLP Act, 2008 requires the name to end with "LLP" or "Limited Liability Partnership".
The line breaks you enter here are kept in the documents.

3. Partner details

For each partner, upload the PAN, Aadhaar and bank statement PDFs and the fields below fill in automatically (shaded fields came from a document). Check every field. The address is taken from the bank statement. Scanned or photographed copies can't be read, so key those details in by hand.
