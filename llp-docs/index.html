LLP Incorporation Documents

Generates the Form 9 consent, consent letter, subscription sheet and NOC for the registered office as Word files. Partner details can be read from PAN, Aadhaar and bank statement PDFs. Everything runs in this browser and nothing is uploaded.

1. Partners in the proposed LLP

An LLP needs at least two partners (Section 6(1)) and at least two designated partners who are individuals, at least one of them resident in India (Section 7(1)), LLP Act, 2008.
Have the details in Excel instead? Useful when the KYC copies are scanned. Download the template, fill it in and upload it. This fills the LLP, partner, NOC and witness details below.

2. LLP details

Section 15(1) of the LLP Act, 2008 requires the name to end with "LLP" or "Limited Liability Partnership".
The line breaks you enter here are kept in the documents.

3. Partner details

For each partner, upload the PAN, Aadhaar and bank statement PDFs and the fields below fill in automatically (shaded fields came from a document). Check every field. The address is taken from the bank statement. Scanned or photographed copies can't be read, so key those details in by hand.

6. LLP Agreement

Draft the objects clause Describe the business and the tool suggests a draft main objects clause, which you can edit here.
Each line becomes a separate paragraph. The first line is also shown as the main business in the data sheet.
Part A of Schedule 1 (ancillary objects) and Schedule 2 are standard text from your format.

7. Checklist

Items received from an upload or entered details tick themselves; tick the rest as they come in. Unticked items go into the data sheet and the request list for the client.

Advanced: use your own edited Word template for this session

Edit a file from the templates folder in Word (keep the {placeholders}) and choose it below. To make the change permanent, save it in the templates folder and run tools/embed_templates.py.
